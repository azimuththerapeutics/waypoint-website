A different kind of practice

A supportive community where people can thrive

Waypoint Cooperative is a clinician-owned mental health practice. Our mission is to empower the people we serve — and the people who work here — toward autonomy and independence through holistic healing.

01

Clinician-Owned

Every provider holds an ownership stake in the practice. No investor board, no volume quotas — the people delivering your care are the same people accountable for its quality.

02

Whole-Person Care

Psychiatry, therapy, EMDR, art therapy, and mindfulness under one roof. Your prescriber and therapist are colleagues who actually talk — integrated care with fewer gaps.

03

Rooted in Torrington

We're based on Main Street and invested in Litchfield County for the long term. Telehealth available across Connecticut. Accepting new clients — no referral needed.

The cooperative model

What “worker-owned” actually means for you

In most practices, business pressure quietly shapes clinical decisions. Waypoint is organized as a cooperative — the clinicians who see you are the same people who set the policies and own the practice. There's no one above them dictating how your appointment should run.

No investor board making decisions about your care
Clinicians stay because they own what they've built
Surplus reinvested in care, not distributed upward

What we offer

Care for where you are

Outpatient care that fits into your life — in person in Torrington or by telehealth across Connecticut. Accepting new clients, with online booking and payments.

01

Mood Disorders

Evidence-based treatment for depression, anxiety, and bipolar spectrum conditions — therapy and medication working together, coordinated by clinicians in the same practice.

02

Trauma & Grief Recovery

EMDR (with EMDRIA-certified and advanced-certified clinicians), Brainspotting, art therapy, and trauma-informed approaches for processing what talk alone can't reach.

03

Medication Management

Psychiatric evaluation and ongoing medication management with our psychiatric nurse practitioner — thoughtful prescribing, close monitoring, real conversations.

04

ADD / ADHD

Comprehensive evaluation and treatment for attention disorders in adults — clarifying the diagnosis first, then building a plan that may include therapy, skills, and medication.

05

Substance Use Recovery

Compassionate, judgment-free care led by licensed alcohol and drug counselors — motivational interviewing, harm reduction, and dual-diagnosis treatment.

06

Group Therapy

Small, clinician-facilitated groups where connection itself is the treatment. Current offerings and openings available when you call.

For clinicians

Grow your practice with us

Supervision, consultation, and training from clinician-owners who've built what they teach — plus a path to cooperative membership.

01

Clinical Supervision

Individual and group supervision with an Approved Clinical Supervisor — for LMSW and LCSW candidates and clinicians building their practice.

02

EMDR Consultation

Individual and group consultation toward EMDRIA certification, with an EMDRIA-Approved Consultant on staff.

03

Clinical Research & Training

Research collaboration and professional training led by our Chief Research Officer, core faculty at Southern Connecticut State University.

04

Health & Wellness

Mindfulness, somatic practices, and yoga-informed programming — integrating a 200-hour trained yoga teacher into clinical care.
